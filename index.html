Welcome to Longmeadow Animal Hospital

Our Services

We have offered the highest quality veterinary medical care to the greater Hagerstown, Smithsburg, Leitersburg area since 1971. We are dedicated to helping our companion animal friends live longer, healthier, and happier lives.

Wellness Care

Annual exams, vaccinations, diet & nutrition, and parasite control to keep pets on track.

Dentistry

Annual dental exams and cleanings under anesthesia, with full-mouth radiographs.

Surgery

Spay & neuter, soft tissue and orthopedic surgery, plus laser surgery options.

Diagnostics

In-house laboratory, radiography and diagnostic ultrasound for fast answers.

Also offering laser therapy, microchipping, parasite control, domestic health certificates, and euthanasia & cremation services.

Meet Your Team

Led by Dr. Rebecca Gurshman (Cornell DVM, Medical Director), our team of 14 brings decades of experience in companion animal medicine and surgery.

Leadership & Veterinarians

The Doctors

DVM, Medical Director

Dr. Rebecca Gurshman

Rebecca Gurshman is a native of New Jersey. She received her B.S. from Cornell University in 1989 and her DVM from Cornell University in 1993. She practiced veterinary medicine in Prince George's County and Calvert County, Maryland for 24 years. Dr. Gurshman joined LAH in 2017 when her family moved to Hagerstown. She and her dogs enjoy long walks on the C&O Canal and exploring local hiking trails.

Hospital Manager

Amy Miller

Amy has been in the veterinary field for about 30 years on & off, starting right out of high school. She previously worked at Longmeadow back in 2008 as a Veterinary Assistant/Surgical Tech, then returned to the field as a Client Service Representative and re-joined the Longmeadow team in 2019 as Office Manager. She then moved into the Assistant Hospital Manager position and is now our Hospital Manager. Amy can still be seen performing Technician and CSR duties from time to time. She enjoys spending time with her fiancé David, her children and her many brothers and sisters (5), reading, camping and relaxing. Amy currently has 2 dogs — Zuma and Riley, 2 cats — Chibi and Roadrunner, a grand pup Finley, and shares custody of her grand-horse Private Pilot.

Associate DVM

Dr. Melissa Horman

Dr. Melissa Horman is a Hagerstown, MD local. She received her Bachelor's degree from Shepherd University and her Doctorate in Veterinary Medicine from Midwestern University College of Veterinary Medicine in Arizona. She has two dogs and one cat. Outside of work she enjoys spending time with her family and friends.

Associate DVM

Dr. Felicia Dryburg

Dr. Felicia Dryburg, a native of West Virginia, has been practicing companion animal medicine and surgery in this region for over 18 years. In 2001, she received her Bachelor of Science degree from Shepherd College, West Virginia. She then went on to receive her Doctorate in Veterinary Medicine (2006) from Tuskegee University in Alabama. She is an active member of the Feline Veterinary Medical Association (FelineVMA), formerly the American Association of Feline Practitioners, and the American Veterinary Medical Association (AVMA). She comes to Longmeadow Animal Hospital with a diverse work history that includes emergency and critical care, preventative and shelter medicine experience. Her special interests are feline medicine, soft tissue surgery, and elimination disorders. In her spare time, she enjoys reading, gardening, knitting, and spending time with her family.
